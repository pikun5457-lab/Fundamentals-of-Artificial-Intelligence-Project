# Human Anatomy Explorer — Medical Student Edition (All 12 Systems)

An interactive 3D human anatomy learning application expanded from your prototype to include **all 12 major body systems** (with the Reproductive System separated into Male and Female systems) and over **100+ distinct anatomical structures** complete with *Terminologia Anatomica* Latin names, muscle origins/insertions/actions/innervations, vascular/lymphatic pathways, histology notes, and USMLE/medical board clinical pearls.

## Included Body Systems (12)
1. **Integumentary System**: Epidermis/dermis/subcutis, scalp & hair follicles, eccrine/apocrine/sebaceous glands, nail plates & nail beds.
2. **Skeletal System**: Neurocranium, viscerocranium/mandible, hyoid bone, cervical (C1–C7), thoracic (T1–T12), lumbar (L1–L5) + intervertebral discs, sacrum & coccyx, sternum & ribs (1–12), clavicle & scapula, humerus, radius & ulna, carpals/metacarpals/phalanges, pelvic girdle, femur, patella & menisci, tibia & fibula, tarsals/metatarsals/phalanges.
3. **Muscular System**: 48+ major head, neck, trunk, rotator cuff, upper limb, gluteal, thigh, and lower leg muscles with full Origin, Insertion, Action, Nerve, and Clinical notes.
4. **Nervous System**: Cerebral hemispheres, cerebellum & brainstem, diencephalon, cranial nerves (CN I–XII / vagus), spinal cord & cauda equina, brachial plexus (median, ulnar, radial), lumbosacral plexus (femoral, sciatic, tibial, common fibular), and sympathetic trunk.
5. **Endocrine System**: Pituitary gland, thyroid & parathyroid glands, adrenal glands, and pancreatic Islets of Langerhans.
6. **Cardiovascular (Circulatory) System**: Heart (chambers, valves, coronary arteries), aorta & systemic arteries (carotid, subclavian, celiac, SMA, renal, iliac, femoral), and vena cavae / hepatic portal / deep & superficial veins.
7. **Lymphatic & Immune System**: Thymus, spleen, Waldeyer tonsillar ring & MALT, thoracic duct, cisterna chyli, right lymphatic duct, and regional lymph node chains.
8. **Respiratory System**: Nasal cavity & paranasal sinuses, larynx, trachea & bronchi, and lungs & pleura.
9. **Digestive System**: Oral cavity, tongue & salivary glands, esophagus, stomach, liver & gallbladder, duodenum & exocrine pancreas, jejunum & ileum, and cecum/appendix/colon/rectum.
10. **Urinary (Excretory) System**: Kidneys, ureters, urinary bladder, and urethra.
11. **Male Reproductive System**: Testes, epididymis, vas deferens/spermatic cord, prostate, seminal vesicles, and bulbourethral glands.
12. **Female Reproductive System**: Ovaries, uterine (fallopian) tubes, uterus, cervix, vagina, and mammary glands.

## How to Run
- **Option 1 (Direct):** Double-click `index.html` to open it directly in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) with an internet connection (for Three.js CDN and Google Fonts).
- **Option 2 (Local Server):**
  ```bash
  python -m http.server 8000
  ```
  Then open `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
